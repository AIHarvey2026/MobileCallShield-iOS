Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 | Mobile Call Shield LLC

1. Overview

Mobile Call Shield ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our call screening services collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application and backend services.

2. Information We Collect

  • Phone Numbers & Call Logs: We process incoming telephone numbers to identify contacts and verify unrecognized callers.
  • Voice & Passphrase Audio: When an unknown caller dials your line, audio passphrases spoken by the caller are processed in real-time solely for automated caller verification and call routing.
  • Device & Contact Data: The application checks saved device contacts locally to allow approved contacts to bypass the call screening step.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is strictly used for functional security operations:

  • To screen unknown callers, request voice passphrases, and prevent automated spam calls.
  • To connect verified incoming calls to your mobile device.
  • To maintain system health and performance monitoring on secure cloud servers.

4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We never sell or rent your personal data to third parties or advertisers. We rely on secure infrastructure providers solely to operate the service:

  • Twilio: Used for telecommunication call routing and speech processing.
  • Render & PostgreSQL: Used for secure cloud hosting and database storage.

5. Data Deletion & User Rights

You retain control over your data. You may request the deletion of your account and any associated screening records at any time by contacting our support team at support@mobilecallshield.com.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy, please contact us at support@mobilecallshield.com.
